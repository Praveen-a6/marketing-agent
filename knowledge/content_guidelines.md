# Content Guidelines

## Brand Position

Career Solutions helps students become job-ready through training, internships, projects, and placement support.

---

## Target Audience

Primary:

* Students
* Fresh Graduates

Secondary:

* HRs
* Companies
* Colleges

---

## Content Pillars

### Career Growth

* Career advice
* Job readiness
* Placement guidance

---

### Technical Learning

* AI
* Data Science
* Data Analytics
* Full Stack Development
* Cybersecurity

---

### Social Proof

* Student success stories
* Placement achievements
* Internship outcomes

---

### Educational Content

* Tips
* Tutorials
* Industry insights

---

## Tone

* Professional
* Helpful
* Encouraging
* Practical

---

## CTA Examples

* Contact us today
* Book a counselling session
* Join our internship program
* Learn more
* Apply now

---

## Avoid

* False promises
* Guaranteed placement claims
* Misleading information
