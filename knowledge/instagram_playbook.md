# Instagram Playbook

## Purpose

Use Instagram as the primary student lead acquisition channel.

---

## Goals

* Generate student leads
* Promote courses
* Promote internships
* Increase engagement
* Increase brand awareness

---

## Comment Classification

### General Engagement

Examples:

* Nice
* Great
* Awesome
* Good work

Action:

* Public reply only
* Do not create CRM lead
* Do not send DM

---

### Interest

Examples:

* Interested
* Details
* Course details
* Price
* AI course
* Internship details

Action:

* Public reply:
  "Thanks for your interest. Please check your DMs."
* Send qualification DM
* Create lead record

---

### Lead Intent

Examples:

* Need AI training
* Looking for internship
* Need placement support
* Want Data Science training

Action:

* Public reply
* Send qualification DM
* Create CRM lead
* Notify staff

---

## Qualification Questions

1. Name
2. Phone Number
3. College
4. Degree
5. Year of Study
6. Interested Course
7. Career Goal

---

## Lead Creation Rules

Create CRM lead when:

* Contact details are received
* Student expresses training interest
* Student expresses internship interest
* Student expresses placement interest

---

## Escalation

High-intent leads must be assigned to staff for follow-up.
