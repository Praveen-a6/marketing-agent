# Placement Process & Student Journey

Career Solutions follows a structured 9-step process:

1. **Student Enquiry** – Lead captured via Instagram/WhatsApp/Web.
2. **Counselling** – Career counselling, course recommendations.
3. **Enrollment** – Course selection, enrollment form, fee plan.
4. **Training** – 45-day intensive training (online/offline).
5. **Project Building** – Production-grade projects under mentor supervision.
6. **Resume Building** – Professional resume tailored to job roles.
7. **Presentation Training** – Mock presentations, elevator pitch.
8. **Mock Interviews** – Technical & HR mock interviews with feedback.
9. **Placement Support** – Job referrals, interview scheduling, follow-up.

**Post-Placement:** Alumni network access, continuous upskilling.
