# Telegram Operations

## Purpose

Telegram is the operational control panel.

---

## Users

### Admin

Permissions:

* Full access
* Approvals
* Reports
* Configuration

---

### Placement Officer

Permissions:

* View assigned leads
* View reports
* Update lead status

---

### Marketing Executive

Permissions:

* View marketing leads
* View campaign reports

---

## Notifications

Send notifications for:

* New leads
* High-priority leads
* Approval requests
* Token budget alerts

---

## Reports

### Daily

* Leads generated
* Leads qualified
* Campaign activity

---

### Weekly

* Lead summary
* Campaign summary
* Conversion metrics

---

## Approval Requests

Required for:

* Sending messages
* External actions
* Major workflow actions
