# Lead Scoring Rules

## Purpose

Score leads for prioritization.

---

## Student Status

Final Year Student = +20

Pre-final Year Student = +10

Graduate = +15

Working Professional = +15

---

## Interest

AI / ML = +15

Data Science = +15

Data Analytics = +10

Full Stack Development = +10

Cybersecurity = +10

---

## Intent

Course Enquiry = +10

Internship Request = +20

Placement Support Request = +25

Requested Counselling = +15

---

## Contact Information

Phone Number Provided = +20

Email Provided = +10

---

## Negative Factors

Spam = -100

Incomplete Information = -10

Invalid Contact Information = -30

---

## Lead Priority

0–20 = Low

21–50 = Medium

51–100 = High
