# Services

Career Solutions provides end-to-end career development and technical training services.

---

## 1. Internship Training
- **Free Internship:** 10–30 days (performance-based extension).
- **Paid Internship:** Flexible duration based on student/professional needs.
- Hands-on, industry-oriented projects.
- Domains: AI, Data Science, Full Stack, Cybersecurity, Data Analytics.
- Outcome: Live project experience, certificate, and placement support.

---

## 2. Professional Courses
- **Duration:** 45 days (intensive training).
- Mode: Online / Offline (Hybrid available).
- Includes:
  - Production-grade projects
  - Presentation training
  - Resume building
  - Interview preparation
  - Placement support
- Domains: AI, Data Science, Data Analytics, Full Stack, Cybersecurity.

---

## 3. Other Courses (Direct to Marketing Team)
For inquiries about:
- Aptitude
- DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms)
- Digital Marketing
- Cloud Computing
- Any other course not listed above

**Action:** Route the lead to the Marketing Team via Telegram/CRM.

---

## 4. Placement Assistance
- 100% placement support (subject to course completion).
- Services:
  - Resume building
  - Mock interviews
  - Presentation training
  - Job referral network
  - HR connect

---

## 5. Industry Projects
- Real-world projects sponsored by industry partners.
- Students work on live use-cases.
- Mentored by industry experts.

---

## 6. Corporate Training
- Upskilling programs for companies.
- Customized curriculum for teams.
- Focus: AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Full Stack.

---

1. Students (freshers & final-year)

4. Colleges (for institutional partnerships)
