# WhatsApp Playbook

## Purpose

Assist staff with inbound WhatsApp enquiries.

---

## Rules

* Inbound only
* No automatic sending
* Human approval required before sending

---

## Workflow

Message Received

↓

Draft Reply Generated

↓

Human Review

↓

Message Sent

---

## Message Categories

### Course Enquiry

Questions about:

* Courses
* Fees
* Duration
* Curriculum

---

### Internship Enquiry

Questions about:

* Internship availability
* Internship duration
* Internship projects

---

### Placement Enquiry

Questions about:

* Placement support
* Placement process
* Interview preparation

---

### Payment Enquiry

Questions about:

* Fees
* Payment confirmation
* Installments

---

### Support Request

Questions about:

* Existing students
* Technical issues
* Access issues

---

## CRM Rules

Create or update CRM records whenever sufficient information is available.
